Valheim Signcarver

Compose an emoji chest label, copy the text, paste it onto a sign in the game. No mods needed: signs already read these tags.

Lines

Emoji you click go to line 1

    Sizes are absolute: 14 fills the plank top to bottom, and lines add up, so 6 for the emoji plus 3 for the word fits. Leave every size blank and the game fits the text to the plank on its own, up to about size 8.

    Emoji

    drawn as the game draws them

    Signs draw emoji from a single-color font, so each one takes the line's color. Hover a tile for the item it stands for. Emoji added in 2024 or later, and combined ones (skin tones, flags, joined pairs), come out as boxes in-game.

    Position

    A sign centers its text on the plank. Blank lines below lift the label; blank lines above push it down. Useful when the sign sits on the ground or on a chest lid.

    Sign text

    0 / 50

    Signs hold 50 characters, tags included. Most emoji count as 2. The sign font is capitals only.

    In game

    1. Copy the sign text.
    2. Place a sign (Hammer → Misc) or press E on an existing one.
    3. Paste with Ctrl+V. The edit box may draw the tags tiny while you type. That is normal.
